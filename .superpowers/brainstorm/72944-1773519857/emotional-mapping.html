Emotional colour mapping

Each audio feature state drives a different plasma palette and behaviour. These are the four key emotional quadrants (Russell's circumplex model — the same framework the audio features use).

HIGH AROUSAL · HIGH VALENCE
Joyful / Euphoric
Warm golds, rose, amber. Fast plasma motion. Frequent bright filaments. Beat rings expand wide and bright. Feels like sunlight through storm clouds.
HIGH AROUSAL · LOW VALENCE
Tense / Intense
Deep crimson, violent purple, ember orange. Erratic fast motion. Many jagged filaments. Plasma field visibly distorted by tension. Feels like pressure building to breaking point.
LOW AROUSAL · HIGH VALENCE
Serene / Peaceful
Soft aquamarine, mint, gentle teal. Very slow plasma drift. Wide gentle beat rings. Few filaments, only on strong transients. Feels like breathing slowly underwater.
LOW AROUSAL · LOW VALENCE
Melancholic / Sorrowful
Deep indigo, dim violet, cold blue. Slow, dim plasma pooling downward. Filaments fall rather than radiate. Beat rings are small and faint. Feels like grief settling into stillness.
Feature → palette mapping:   valence rotates hue warm↔cool  ·  tension injects turbulence & red shift  ·  arousal drives animation speed  ·  rms controls brightness  ·  buildupIntensity warps the field geometry

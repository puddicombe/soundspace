What world should this visualizer inhabit?

The aesthetic foundation shapes everything — motion, colour, emotion. Which direction resonates?

Cosmic / Celestial

Planets, nebulae, orbital mechanics. Tension pulls orbits toward collapse; joy scatters stardust. Music becomes a gravitational force shaping the cosmos.

Living Organism

Mycelium, neurons, coral. The music breathes through branching structures — soft passages grow slowly, percussion fires synapses, dissonance causes the organism to recoil.

Fluid Dynamics

Ink in water, lava lamp, interference waves. Sound creates pressure waves that visibly disturb a fluid medium — calm music produces gentle eddies, intense music causes turbulent collisions.

Sacred Geometry

Mandalas, Lissajous figures, cymatics. Harmony produces beautiful symmetrical forms; dissonance shatters them into chaos. The visual directly embodies mathematical relationships in the music.

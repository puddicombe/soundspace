What kind of fluid?

Three different visual registers within fluid dynamics — each creates a very different emotional texture

Ink in Water

Dark pigment on pale ground — or luminous colour on black. Drops bloom and diffuse slowly. Each beat injects new ink; valence shifts the pigment hue. Feels like watching thought or memory spread.

Plasma / Aurora

Luminous gases on a deep black void. Colours shift with emotional valence — warm for happy/major, violet-blue for melancholic/minor. High tension creates electric filaments; the drop hits like a solar flare.

Metaball / Lava Lamp

Slow organic blobs that attract and merge when close, repel and split when tense. Pitch determines which blobs are drawn together — notes in harmony merge; dissonance pushes them apart. Hypnotic and unsettling.

How does the tunnel behave as the music changes?
A — Straight, always ahead
Clean, locked-forward flight. The grid is the show — no camera deviation. Calm music = faint grid, intense music = blazing grid.
B — Banks & curves with music
Vanishing point drifts left/right/up/down based on audio. Feels like the ship is banking through the canyon. Driven by spectral tilt or low-freq balance.
C — Straight with warp on drops
Normally straight-ahead, but on high-energy moments the tunnel twists/compresses (barrel distortion on the grid). Like hitting a jump to lightspeed burst.
